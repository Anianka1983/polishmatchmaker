Dating in London as a Pole: Why Apps Are Not Always Enough

London is a city of enormous possibility, yet meeting someone who truly fits can be surprisingly hard.

By Ania (Anna), Dobrani · 20 Aug 2026

A big city and little time

Many people who have moved to London work long hours and have little time for a social life. Commuting, work and everyday obligations mean there are fewer chances to meet someone in a relaxed way than you might expect.

Apps promise convenience, but browsing profiles and keeping several conversations going at once takes its own time and energy.

No shared context

People who grew up in Poland or in a Polish family often look for someone who understands their values and cultural background. Apps rarely let you judge that early on, so it is easy to meet someone with whom there is no basic common ground.

That does not mean a partner must be Polish. It means being clear about what matters to you.

App fatigue

It is a common experience that conversations fade without explanation and dates lead nowhere lasting. After a while, many people start looking for another way.

One option is matchmaking: a personal conversation, thoughtful matching and an introduction to someone who is serious about a relationship.

What you can do

It helps to define clearly what you are looking for and to ask whether searching alone is still working for you. If you would like to talk about an alternative, you are welcome to book a free, confidential conversation.
