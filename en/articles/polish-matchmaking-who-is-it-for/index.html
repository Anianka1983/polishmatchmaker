Polish Matchmaking: What It Is and Who It Is For

Matchmaking is an older and more personal idea than it may seem: instead of browsing profiles alone, you entrust the search to someone who gets to know you properly.

By Ania (Anna), Dobrani · 2026-10-04

What private matchmaking is

Private matchmaking is a service in which a matchmaker learns your expectations, values and lifestyle, then looks for people who genuinely fit them. It is not a catalogue of profiles or an algorithm. Behind every introduction there is a conversation and human judgement.

A good matchmaker does not promise miracles. Her job is to save you the time you would otherwise spend meeting people who were never likely to be right.

Why “Polish”

For many people with Polish roots, talking about relationships, family and expectations feels more natural in their first language. A shared cultural context, from holiday traditions to attitudes towards family, helps you explain what you are looking for more quickly.

At Dobrani, conversations are held in Polish or English, whichever you prefer, and the search covers London and the whole UK.

Who matchmaking is for

It is most often used by busy people who do not want to spend their evenings on apps, and by those who have been disappointed before and are looking for a serious relationship. It also suits people who value discretion and prefer not to put their profile on public display.

If you are not sure it is the right route, the first conversation is free and without obligation. It is a good chance to ask questions and judge for yourself whether the approach suits you.

What working together looks like

It begins with an initial conversation, followed by thoughtful matching and, finally, a discreet introduction. We describe each step on the “How it works” page.
