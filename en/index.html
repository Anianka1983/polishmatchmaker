Dobrani (doh-BRAH-nee) - Polish for “the well-matched”: people who suit one another.

Why a Polish matchmaker

Living between two cultures has its challenges - in love as well

Many people with Polish roots move between two languages, two sets of expectations and often two families, each with its own idea of the ideal partner. Dating apps do not account for any of that. A good matchmaker does.

A conversation in your own language

Your first conversation takes place in Polish or English, whichever feels more natural. It is easier to talk about values, family and expectations when you do not have to search for the words.

Matching with care

I combine personality compatibility with personal judgement and a trusted network. I only get in touch when there is someone genuinely aligned with you, not simply available.

Discretion and respect

Your details are handled discreetly and never shared without consent. Kindness and respect are non-negotiable for everyone in my network.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Polish?

No. I hold consultations in Polish or English. People with Polish roots who grew up in the UK are just as welcome.

Will I only meet people of Polish background?

If that matters to you, I take it into account when matching. If you are open to a wider circle, I can draw on the whole Sparked Connection network. During our conversation I will be honest about what the network currently looks like in your area, without making promises I cannot keep.

How much does it cost?

The initial consultation is free and without obligation. Bronze membership (access to the Singles Network) is free, while Gold and Platinum are paid packages. You will find the details on the Services page.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your details are handled discreetly and are never shared without your consent. Matchmaking is a personal process, and confidentiality is core to how I work.

Do you only work in London?

I work with people in London and across the whole of the UK. We can agree whichever format suits you best for the first conversation.

What is the first conversation like?

A calm, confidential one-to-one conversation with no pressure. We talk about your values, your lifestyle and what a good relationship looks like to you. Only then do we decide together whether and how to go further.
