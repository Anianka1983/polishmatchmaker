About me

Ania, founder and lead matchmaker

or simply Anna, to my English-speaking clients

The person behind every match.

How it began

For years I worked with driven, high-achieving professionals and kept seeing the same thing: people who had built successful careers felt stuck in their personal lives. Not because they were not trying. Swiping between meetings is not dating, and no app was built for people with no time to waste on the wrong person.

I had been matchmaking for a long time, informally, among friends and family, with several successful matches. When I saw how many people around me struggled with dating apps, I decided to do it professionally.

Every person I introduce is vetted and sincere about finding a real relationship. Kindness and respect are non-negotiable.

As a Polish woman living in the UK, I know what it is to bring two worlds together. That is why I want people with Polish roots to have a matchmaker they can talk to in their own language, and who understands what they are looking for.

Ania
Where the name comes from

Dobrani

In Polish, “dobrani” means “the well-matched”: people chosen with care because they suit one another. Pronounced doh-BRAH-nee. “Ania” is the Polish form of Anna, which is how my English-speaking clients know me.

My approach

Three principles

Involvement

This is not an app quietly running in the background of your life. It is me, involved at every step, doing the work an algorithm never could.

Respect

Kindness and respect are conditions for everyone in my network. No ghosting, no games.

Discretion

Your details are handled in confidence and never shared without consent. Matchmaking is a personal process and I treat it as one.
